About Directions

Directions is a creative writing game.

You can choose how to interpret the prompts it will provide to you. It works best when you come to it with a poetic disposition.

It's your choice to approach the prompts however you like. Your writing may be altered by the various text editors that you encounter. The game works best when you 'write for' the changing medium and allow it to shape the direction you pursue.
