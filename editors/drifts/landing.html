About Drifts

Drifts is a creative writing game.

The game is intended to be used as a way to disrupt your own creative writing practice. Your experience will be shaped by your interpretation of the prompts. You will have a different experience if you know what to expect, so you may want to try a few runs before committing to each page.

It's your choice to approach the prompts however you like. Your writing may be altered by the various text editors that you encounter. The game works best when you 'write for' the changing medium and allow it to shape the drift you pursue.

Data Policy

No data is sent to any servers. Gameplay state is saved locally in the browser's local storage. To avoid losing your creations, download your save file regularly.
