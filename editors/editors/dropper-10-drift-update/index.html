How to Play

Gameplay

Write the best poem you can with the words available!

A line of your poem is complete when the bottom most word in each column matches its 'constraint'.

Words, constraints, and delete blocks will appear in the queue.

Controls

Left/Right Arrow Keys: Move the queue of words left and right

Down Arrow or Space:Drop the current block in its current position. Current block is indicated by the three dots.

Touch/Swipe: Swipe left/right to move, swipe down to drop

Block Types

Word: A word from the source text that can be used to complete a line

Constraint:A property that must be matched to complete a line. These appear as colored underlines, and can be dropped just like a word. Constraints labels will disappear to indicate that they have been matched. Currently these are parts of speech like nouns, verbs, adjectives, etc. There is also a special 'word' constraint that will match any word.

Delete: A block that will delete the bottom most word in a column
